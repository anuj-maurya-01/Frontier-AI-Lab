## 6. Reproducibility and access notes

Paper links identify scholarly sources; they do not transfer a license to reuse the papers’ text or figures. This project paraphrases findings and does not reproduce paper figures. Package licenses above are recorded from their published package metadata; `package-lock.json` is the versioned installation record. Browser-rendered PDFs use system/browser fonts with CSS fallbacks if remote Google Fonts are unavailable.
